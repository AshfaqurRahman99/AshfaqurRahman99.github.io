Workshop on Computational Fluid Dynamics
Organized by: IMechE KUET Student Chapter • 2024

In 2024, I participated in an intensive, week-long workshop on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), organized by the IMechE KUET Student Chapter. Using industry-standard Ansys software, the program provided a practical introduction to fluid flow and heat transfer simulations. Throughout the workshop, I gained hands-on insight into the standard CFD pipeline: from geometry preparation and mesh generation within the Ansys environment to setting up physics-based boundary conditions, configuring solvers, and analyzing the results through post-processing. This experience strengthened my engineering toolkit, giving me a foundational understanding of how to virtually simulate and optimize real-world fluid systems.

Industrial Attachment (Production Dept.)
Bangladesh Steel Re-Rolling Mills (BSRM) • 2023

During my time in the steel manufacturing industry, I gained comprehensive, hands-on experience in heavy industrial processes, with a specialized focus on billet manufacturing and advanced furnace operations. Working within a leading steel production environment, I actively analyzed real-world quality control systems and industrial management practices. My technical training spanned the entire production cycle—from scrap processing and Induction Furnace (IF) operations to managing precise chemical mixing ratios and Ladle Refining Furnace (LRF) processes. Additionally, I developed a strong foundational understanding of Continuous Casting Machine (CCM) operations, industrial cooling mechanisms, and strict health, safety, and environmental protocols, including air pollution control and water treatment systems.
